## A note on how this document came to be

This draft was AI-assisted, written to be genuinely protective and honest
about what Centsible currently is — a free, individually-run, pre-revenue
project. It is **not a substitute for review by a California-licensed
attorney**, and that review becomes a real priority (not optional
box-checking) at any of these points:

- Before you charge money for the app in any form
- Before the user base grows meaningfully past friends and testers
- Before or alongside forming an LLC or corporation — the entity formation
  and these Terms should be reviewed together, since the whole point of
  incorporating is to shift liability off you personally, and that only
  works if the Terms and the entity are set up consistently
- If you ever face an actual dispute — §13's arbitration clause in
  particular is the kind of clause that gets challenged in court, and its
  enforceability depends on details worth having a lawyer confirm before
  you need to rely on it

At today's scale, this draft is a reasonable, good-faith document to have
in place. It is not the same thing as a lawyer's sign-off, and shouldn't be
mistaken for one once real money or real scale enters the picture.
